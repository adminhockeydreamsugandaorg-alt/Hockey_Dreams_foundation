Who We Are

Hockey Dreams Uganda (HDFU) is a youth-focused non-governmental organisation that uses field hockey to promote sports development, personal growth, and inclusive opportunities for children and young people.

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to discover their abilities, develop confidence, learn valuable life skills, and experience the benefits of participating in sport.

Through structured hockey training, school programmes, holiday training camps, tournaments, and community activities, we create opportunities for young people to learn the game, develop their potential, and build positive relationships.

Our Mission

To use field hockey as a tool for youth development, expanding access to sport while equipping children and young people with valuable life skills, confidence, discipline, and a spirit of teamwork.

Our Vision

To contribute to a future where children and young people have equal opportunities to participate in sport, develop their abilities, and reach their potential, while field hockey continues to grow and thrive in Uganda.

What We Do

Field Hockey Development

We provide structured training opportunities that help young players develop hockey skills, understand the game, improve teamwork, and grow in confidence.

School Programmes

We engage with schools to introduce and develop field hockey, encourage student participation, and create opportunities to learn through organised sport.

Holiday Camps and Tournaments

Our camps and tournaments give young people opportunities to practise their skills, experience healthy competition, build friendships, and enjoy sport.

Life Skills and Inclusion

We use sport to encourage leadership, communication, respect, responsibility, perseverance, and opportunities for children and young people to participate.

Our Core Values

Respect

We value others and treat participants, coaches, families, and partners fairly.

Teamwork

We believe in cooperation, mutual support, and working towards shared goals.

Discipline

We encourage commitment, responsibility, consistency, and respect for the game.

Inclusion

We promote opportunities for children and young people to participate in sport.

Integrity

We value honesty, fairness, accountability, and good sportsmanship.

Growth

We encourage learning, personal development, and continuous improvement.

Our Commitment

At Hockey Dreams Uganda, we believe sport can open doors, build character, and bring communities together. We are committed to supporting the growth of field hockey, promoting the development of young people, and encouraging inclusive opportunities to participate in sport.

We welcome collaboration with schools, families, community organisations, sponsors, donors, and partners who share our commitment to youth development through sport.
